WHY CARESYNQ

Support should notice the mother, too.

The weeks after childbirth can bring major changes. CARESYNQ is designed to make everyday tracking easier and help care teams identify sustained changes that may need a human check-in.

01

Capture gently

Mothers can choose to record simple daily activity, feeding and sleep notes.

02

Understand change

The prototype compares patterns with an individual's own recent baseline and checks whether changes persist.

03

Connect to care

Any alert is a prompt for authorised human review—not a diagnosis or treatment decision.

Privacy and choice

Participation should be voluntary. Collect only the information needed, explain how it is used, restrict access, and allow users to withdraw. This demo uses sample data and is not intended for real clinical decisions.
